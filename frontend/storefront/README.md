## Discover products (Cart, Wishlist, Checkout)

Obviously as any ecommerce website you have shop page where you can discover products of the store , search and filter for what you need , **you can use wishlist** or **directly add them to cart** and finalize your order by **Checking out**

### Products

![Products](/assets/readme-assets/products.png)

### Filter Products

Using **Filter and Sort** button you can filter for products by :

- Availability
- Title
- Price
- Author
- Genre
- Format
- Language

![Products filtering](/assets/readme-assets/filter-products.png)

### Searching Products

You can also use the search bar to search for any book you want and from there directly add it to wishlist or cart

![Search](/assets/readme-assets/searching-products.png)

### Single Product

You can also on the **Single Product** page either buy the product directly _Adding to cart directly and checkout_ or you can add it to cart , in both situation you can set the quantity.

![Single product](/assets/readme-assets/single-product.png)
